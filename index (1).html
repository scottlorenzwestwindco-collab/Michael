About Michael

Strategy built around your book, not a template.

Publishing a great book is only the beginning. Reaching the right readers takes strategy, consistency, and a real understanding of today's publishing landscape — that's where I come in.

I work with both independent and traditionally published authors to build customized marketing plans that improve discoverability and strengthen author brand: Amazon SEO and A+ Content, Goodreads Listopia and community campaigns, digital and social media marketing, reader lead generation, book funnels, author branding, and publisher query support. Every book has its own audience, and every plan is built to connect that book with the readers most likely to love it.

I've supported authors across fantasy, romance, thrillers, mysteries, historical fiction, science fiction, nonfiction, and literary fiction. My mission is simple: help great books get discovered, and give authors the marketing support they need to grow with confidence.
